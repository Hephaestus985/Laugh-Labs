# Laugh-Labs

Silly little Godot games. Download an APK on your Android phone, open it, and allow installing from your browser if Android asks.

| Game | What it is | Android |
|---|---|---|
| Sock Escape | A sock flees the washing machine. Jump ducks, soap and cats, duck under bubbles. | [SockEscape.apk](https://github.com/Hephaestus985/Laugh-Labs/raw/main/apks/SockEscape.apk) |

To play on a PC, open a game folder (e.g. `sock-escape/`) in Godot 4 and press F5.
